# Session 1 · Follow along: your inbox becomes a task list

**15 minutes.** Sort a real inbox into four piles with AI, catch the one it gets wrong, and leave with a to-do list you can paste into a spreadsheet.

**You need:** This demo, open in a browser; An AI chat you are signed in to (Claude or ChatGPT); Optional: 10 of your own emails.

| At | Minutes | Do this | You should see | Tip |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | 1 | Open the demo (double-click Launch) and click Week 1. | Greenline Landscaping's inbox: 12 emails in a table. | Everything here is made up. Your own data never leaves your browser. |
| 0:01 | 2 | Read the inbox like an owner. Count the emails that truly need you today. | Most people count three or four out of twelve. | Say your number out loud or type it in the chat before going on. |
| 0:03 | 2 | Go to step 2, Give the AI the job. Find the job, the rules and the check inside the prompt. | Sort into four piles; never quote a price; never promise a date; ask if unsure. | This is the new-hire rule. Change one line so it sounds like your business. |
| 0:05 | 3 | Press Copy the prompt and the emails. Paste into your AI chat and send. Copy its whole answer. | Four headings (REPLY TODAY, CAN WAIT, FYI, JUNK) and a TASKS list. | No AI chat handy? Use the button that loads the sample answer. |
| 0:08 | 2 | Paste the answer into step 3 and press Use this answer. | Four piles appear, each email as a card. | If the piles do not appear, ask the AI to start each line with EMAIL and its number. |
| 0:10 | 2 | Which one is wrong? Look in FYI for the QuickBooks notice about three overdue invoices. Move it. Then add a rule about money owed to you. | The moved card turns amber, and your rule appears in the prompt. | The AI could not know what a late invoice costs you until you told it. |
| 0:12 | 2 | Scroll to Your list for today. Tick one task done, then press Copy for Google Sheets and paste it into a blank sheet. | A table: task, who, due. | Every task starts with a verb. That is the difference between an inbox and a list. |
| 0:14 | 1 | Click Home. | The first number on your one screen: emails that need a reply today. | Next Wednesday the second tile lights up: a lead form that fills the list by itself. |

## Now with your own business

- Tomorrow morning: press Use my own emails and paste ten of yours, with a blank line between them. Leave out anything with a password or a card number.
- Add one rule that is true only for your business, such as which customers always come first.
- Save your finished prompt somewhere you will find it. It is now your inbox instruction.

## If something goes wrong

- The AI is slow or down: load the sample answer and keep going. The lesson is the checking, not the typing.
- The piles look wrong after pasting: the answer needs each line to start with EMAIL and its number. Ask the AI to redo it in that shape.
- Start over with the sample data puts everything back.
