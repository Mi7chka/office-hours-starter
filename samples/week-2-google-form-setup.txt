SAMPLE DATA. How to build the Week 2 lead form for real, with a Google Form and a Google Sheet.
Greenline Landscaping is a made-up company. No real people.
Used in Wednesday Office Hours, Session 2. The leads are in samples/week-2-sample-leads.csv. The prompt is in prompts/week-2-lead-reply-prompt.txt.
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHAT IT DOES
One sentence: when a lead fills out the form, save it, and tell me.
  Trigger: a Google Form is submitted.
  Action:  a new row lands in a Google Sheet.
  Check:   a notice email arrives.
  Then:    the new row is pasted into an AI chat, which drafts the reply. Nothing is sent.

1. THE FORM
Title:        Greenline Landscaping: request a quote (SAMPLE)
Description:  Sample form for a class. Greenline Landscaping is a made-up company.
Questions, in this order, so the Sheet columns match samples/week-2-sample-leads.csv:
  1. Name                     short answer, required
  2. Email                    short answer, required
  3. Phone                    short answer, not required (lead 6 has none, on purpose)
  4. Service needed           dropdown with these seven choices:
                                Lawn care (weekly)
                                Fall cleanup
                                Patio or hardscape
                                Hedges and mulch
                                Drainage
                                Commercial property
                                Something else
  5. Details                  paragraph
  6. Best time to reach you   short answer
Check that the form does not ask the person to sign in.
The form inside the demo has a seventh question, How did you find us? Add it as a dropdown if you want to know where your leads come from.

2. THE TWO SETTINGS (these are the whole automation)
  a. In the form's Responses area, look for the option that links the form to a new Google Sheet.
     Name the Sheet: Greenline leads (SAMPLE)
  b. In the same area, look for the setting that emails you each time there is a new response. Turn it on.

3. THE TEST RUN
  a. Open the form's fill-out page and submit leads 1, 2 and 3 from the CSV, one at a time.
     Leads 2 and 3 are the same person twice. That is the duplicate.
  b. Confirm three rows in the Sheet, each with a time in the first column.
  c. Confirm three notice emails. Note how long each one took to arrive.
  d. Submit lead 4, Tomas Reyes. He is the 9 PM lead.
  e. Copy the header row and Tomas's row from the Sheet. Paste the prompt, then the two rows, into an AI chat.
  f. Read the draft. Ask which part is wrong. Send nothing.

THE OTHER ROWS
Lead 5 is one more ordinary lead. Lead 7 is too.
Lead 6 has no phone number, on purpose: the draft should not invent one.
Lead 8 is a junk entry, to show what form spam looks like.

WITH YOUR OWN BUSINESS
Change the title, the description and the service choices to yours. Keep the two settings.
Test it with a made-up customer before a real one touches it.
Write three lines and keep them where the next person can find them: what starts it, what it does, who gets told.
Do not put real customer information into the sample form or the sample Sheet.
