SAMPLE DATA. Summary formulas for the Session 7 demo (Greenline Landscaping, a made-up company). No real people.
Before you paste: the clean table sits on a tab named Clean, headers in row 1, columns in this order:
Date, Customer, Service, Town, Amount (USD), Paid, How they found us.
Copy everything below the line and paste it into cell A1 of the tab named Summary. The columns are separated by tabs.
With the sample file you should see: all services 26655 and 29 jobs, unpaid 8775 on 5 jobs, 1 job with no amount.
For the chart: select the block from "Service" down to "Lawn care" (two columns) and look for chart in the menu.

Greenline Landscaping: October on one screen (SAMPLE DATA)

Service	Revenue (USD)	Jobs
Patio and hardscape	=SUMIF(Clean!C:C,A4,Clean!E:E)	=COUNTIF(Clean!C:C,A4)
Mulch and planting	=SUMIF(Clean!C:C,A5,Clean!E:E)	=COUNTIF(Clean!C:C,A5)
Fall cleanup	=SUMIF(Clean!C:C,A6,Clean!E:E)	=COUNTIF(Clean!C:C,A6)
Hedge trimming	=SUMIF(Clean!C:C,A7,Clean!E:E)	=COUNTIF(Clean!C:C,A7)
Lawn care	=SUMIF(Clean!C:C,A8,Clean!E:E)	=COUNTIF(Clean!C:C,A8)
All services	=SUM(Clean!E:E)	=COUNTA(Clean!B:B)-1

Unpaid	Amount (USD)	Jobs
Unpaid, amount known	=SUMIF(Clean!F:F,"No",Clean!E:E)	=COUNTIFS(Clean!F:F,"No",Clean!E:E,">0")
Any job with no amount		=COUNTA(Clean!B:B)-1-COUNT(Clean!E:E)

How they found us	Jobs
Repeat customer	=COUNTIF(Clean!G:G,A16)
Referral	=COUNTIF(Clean!G:G,A17)
Google search	=COUNTIF(Clean!G:G,A18)
Website form	=COUNTIF(Clean!G:G,A19)
Yard sign	=COUNTIF(Clean!G:G,A20)
