THE ONE-PAGE "WHEN IT BREAKS" SHEET
Used in Wednesday Office Hours, Session 8.
Part 1 is the blank sheet. Part 2 is the same sheet filled in for Greenline Landscaping.
Part 2 is SAMPLE DATA: a made-up company, made-up people and made-up vendor contacts.

THE FIVE HEADINGS (paste this one line into the Meet chat)
Tool | Who owns the login | Support contact | While it's down | Renews

HOW TO SET IT UP IN GOOGLE DOCS (about 3 minutes)
1. Open a blank Google Doc. Call it: When it breaks.
2. Look for the table option in the Insert menu. Make 5 columns, with one row for the headings and one row for each tool.
3. Type the five headings across the top row. Type one tool at the start of each row.
4. Fill in the rest as you find the answers. An empty cell is useful. It shows you what nobody knows yet.
5. Under the table, add the "top of the page" lines and the log from Part 1.
6. Print one copy. Keep it where the person who is there at 4:45 on a Friday can find it.

THE ONE RULE
Passwords are never written on this page. They live in the password manager.
This page says whose account it is and who to call.

Before you call anyone, write down five things:
   1. What exactly do you see? The exact words, the time, what you were doing.
   2. Is it one person or everyone?
   3. What changed?
   4. Can you make it happen again?
   5. What does it stop?
Then one fix at a time.
